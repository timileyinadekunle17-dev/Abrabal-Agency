Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Abrabal Agency (“Abrabal Agency”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you visit our website, contact us or enquire about our services.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact information: your name, email address, telephone number, business name and the details you choose to provide in an enquiry.
  • Project information: information about your business, website, goals, requested services, budget or project requirements that you share with us.
  • Communications: messages, feedback, files and other information you send to us by email, WhatsApp or another communication channel.
  • Technical information: basic information that may be provided by your browser or hosting environment, such as device type, browser type, pages requested and general diagnostic logs.

Please do not send passwords, payment card details or other highly sensitive information through public enquiry forms or ordinary email.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, discuss and deliver requested services, prepare proposals, communicate about projects, maintain and improve our website, protect the website from misuse, keep appropriate business records and meet legal obligations.

3. Lawful handling of information

Where applicable, we handle personal information on an appropriate legal basis, such as taking steps at your request before entering a contract, performing an agreement, meeting a legal obligation, protecting legitimate business interests or obtaining your consent where consent is required. We aim to handle information fairly, for clear purposes and only to the extent reasonably necessary.

4. Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with service providers who help us operate our website or communicate with you, such as website hosting providers, email services and messaging platforms. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a lawful business reorganisation. Service providers may process information under their own privacy terms.

5. International processing

Some service providers may store or process information outside Nigeria. Where this occurs, we will take steps required by applicable data protection law for any relevant transfer and use appropriate safeguards where required.

6. Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. We keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose collected, our business records, dispute resolution or legal requirements, and then take reasonable steps to delete or securely dispose of it.

7. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing, withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent, and information about how your data is handled. You may also have the right to complain to the relevant data protection authority. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request and some rights may be limited by law.

8. Cookies and external links

Our website may use essential technical functions provided by the website platform or hosting environment. If we introduce analytics, advertising or other non essential cookies, we will provide any notices or choices required by applicable law. Our website may link to third party websites and services. We do not control their content or privacy practices, so please review their policies before sharing information with them.

9. Children

Our services and website are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when this version was published. Please review this page periodically.

11. Contact us about privacy

For privacy questions or to make a data request, contact Abrabal Agency at abrabalagency@gmail.com or through our WhatsApp contact.
